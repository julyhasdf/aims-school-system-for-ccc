# AIMS Future Roadmap: Suggested Enhancements

To further evolve the Academic Information Management System (AIMS) into a comprehensive school ecosystem, here are several high-value features recommended for future development:

---

### 1. Parent/Guardian Portal
*   **What it is:** A dedicated login for parents to monitor their child's school life.
*   **Value:** Parents can view grades, attendance, and school announcements in real-time, fostering better communication between the school and home.

### 2. Advanced Attendance System
*   **What it is:** A dedicated interface for teachers to take daily attendance using simple checkboxes or QR codes.
*   **Value:** Automates the "Attendance Summary" report and can trigger instant notifications to parents if a student is marked "Absent" or "Late."

### 3. Messaging & Collaboration Center
*   **What it is:** An internal messaging system (like a mini-chat or email inbox) within the system.
*   **Value:** Allows students to ask teachers questions privately and enables administrators to send direct messages to specific groups (e.g., all Grade 10 teachers).

### 4. Learning Management System (LMS) Integration
*   **What it is:** A space for teachers to upload learning materials like PDFs, videos, and worksheets.
*   **Value:** Students can download study guides and review lessons at home, turning the system into a 24/7 learning hub.

### 5. Automated SMS/Email Alerts
*   **What it is:** Integration with a messaging service to send alerts directly to mobile phones.
*   **Value:** Crucial for emergency announcements, payment reminders, or critical academic warnings that need immediate attention.

### 6. School Fees & Payment Tracking
*   **What it is:** A module to track tuition fees, miscellaneous expenses, and payment history.
*   **Value:** Helps the administration manage school finances and allows parents to see their remaining balance and download digital receipts.

### 7. Digital Library & Resource Management
*   **What it is:** A system to track school library books, computer lab equipment, or sports gear.
*   **Value:** Prevents loss of school property and allows students to check if a specific book is available before visiting the library.

### 8. Student Portfolio & Achievements
*   **What it is:** A digital "scrapbook" where students can upload their best projects, certificates, and awards.
*   **Value:** Creates a lasting record of a student’s growth and can be exported as a professional profile when they graduate.

### 9. Interactive School Calendar
*   **What it is:** A visual calendar on the dashboard showing upcoming exams, holidays, and school events.
*   **Value:** Helps everyone stay organized and aware of important dates without having to check multiple announcement posts.

### 10. Predictive Performance Analytics
*   **What it is:** Using data to identify students who might be struggling before they fail.
*   **Value:** The system could flag students whose grades are trending downwards, allowing teachers to step in and offer help early.
